I can see why. Your headline is doing two jobs at once: greeting and describing. Visitors don't need to be greeted — they need to know what you do and why they should care, in under 5 seconds.

Here's a quick framework I'd suggest:
  1. Lead with the outcome, not the company name. ("Ship features 3× faster" beats "Welcome to Acme")
  2. Add a specific number when possible. Conversion lift from vague → quantified copy is consistent across A/B tests.
  3. Match the CTA to the headline. If the headline promises speed, the button should say "Start free" not "Learn more".

<h1>Welcome to Acme. We build software.</h1>

// After
